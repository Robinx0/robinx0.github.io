## About me

I work on the red team at **Shorborno Holdings Ltd** on the **Grameenphone** project - Bangladesh's largest telecommunications company - focusing on adversary simulation, Active Directory exploitation, EDR/AV evasion, and custom offensive tooling in C/C++ and Python. Before Shorborno I was a Security Researcher at PentesterSpace working across mobile, network, and AD pentesting.

I hold **CRTO**, **CPTS**, **eWPTX**, **eCPPT**, and **eJPT**, and have completed the HackTheBox **Dante**, **Rastalabs**, and **Ascension** Pro Labs.
